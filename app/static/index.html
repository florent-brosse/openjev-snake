How it works

      
    
        
  1. Perception (code). Every ~0.5 s the app describes the arena to OpenJev in plain English: your head, rivals, a 9×9 local view, and a list of candidate intents with computed facts (path length, who wins the race, open space, risks).
    2. 
        
  2. Decision (OpenJev). One call to the Databricks Unity AI Gateway route /ai-gateway/typesafe/v1/systemone with model system.ai.openjev-qwen35-4b answers three typed questions at once: intent (choice), outlook (score: losing / even / winning) and danger (noul = probability of yes). No text is generated — just probabilities.
    3. 
        
  3. Action (code). A deterministic controller (BFS + flood-fill anti-trap) executes the chosen intent on every tick. When danger > 0.6, it switches to strict safety mode — decisions are combined in code.
    4. 
        
  4. Data (Delta). Every decision (state, all option probabilities, choice, latency) and every game event lands in Unity Catalog tables, ready for dashboards, evaluation and audits.
    5.
